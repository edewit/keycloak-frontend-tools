## Beyond the Redirect: Modernizing Keycloak for the TypeScript Era

For years, Keycloak has been the enterprise identity provider — secure, battle-tested, but often disconnected from the modern frontend developer's workflow. New auth libraries promise "easy auth" by running inside your app, but they frequently sacrifice the hardened security of a standalone IAM.

In this session I'll show how we're bridging that gap. The talk covers the new OAuth 2.1 Native App Flow and headless UI patterns that let developers build pixel-perfect, framework-agnostic authentication — without raw passwords or clunky redirects.

And I'll demo the open source projects behind it:

- [**keycloak-headless**](../../projects/keycloak-headless/) - Lit web components and framework helpers for React, Vue, Svelte, and Solid
- [**login-ui-base**](../../projects/login-ui-base/) - Modern login themes with client-side or Qute server-side rendering
- [**Backend-for-Frontend**](../../projects/backend-for-frontend/) - Keycloak BFF extension with secure session cookies

Together, these tools cover the full frontend auth stack while keeping Keycloak's multi-realm isolation, OIDC compliance, and enterprise-grade architecture.
